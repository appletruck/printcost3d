Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

PrintCost 3D Pro respects your privacy.

Information Collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. No account registration is required.

Local Data Storage

Saved projects, printer profiles, business names and customer names entered in the app are stored locally on your device. Data may be included in device backups depending on your Apple settings.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify purchase entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

PDF Quotations

PDF quotations are generated locally. Information is shared with other apps or recipients only when you choose to share it.

Advertising and Tracking

The app does not contain advertisements, third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Data Deletion

Saved projects and printer profiles can be deleted within the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups and separately exported files.

Support Communications

If you contact us by email, your email address and message are used to respond to your support request.

Website Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub. GitHub may process technical information when you visit this website.
